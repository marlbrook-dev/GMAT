How the Dates Are Set

Each task carries a lead time measured in days before your deadline, and the planner subtracts it. The lead times come from constraints that are documented rather than invented:

  • Test dates sit furthest out because a retake is not instant. Build in time for one retake and for scores to reach schools.
  • Recommenders need a month, not a week. The ask goes out well before the deadline, with the reminder scheduled separately so you are not chasing in the final fortnight.
  • Transcripts travel slowly, and from some countries they travel very slowly. International applicants get a longer lead time on transcripts and evaluations for that reason.
  • The essay is not one task. First draft, rest, revise, and final read are separate rows, because treating them as one is how applicants end up submitting a first draft.
A word on what this is not. This is a planning tool, not admissions advice, and it does not know any school's actual deadline. Confirm every date on the school's own admissions page. If you are applying from outside the United States, read the international applicant guide for the visa and work authorization rules that sit behind several of these tasks.
