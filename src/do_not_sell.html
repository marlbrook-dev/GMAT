Legal

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

California law gives you the right to tell us not to sell or share your personal information. This page is that control. You do not need an account to read it and you will not be asked to create one.

What We Actually Sell or Share

One category, from one group of people: adults with an account. If you are 18 or over and signed up outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, this is on unless you turn it off, which is what the button below does. If you signed up in one of those places, it is off until you switch it on yourself. Either way, what may be shared is the profile details you chose to fill in: education level, intended major, target schools, score goal, application year, budget band, employer industry, household income band, first-generation status, military status, weekly study hours, which exam you are preparing for, and your country. Recipients may include data brokers.

Where we have bought details about you from a data partner, which we do only on accounts recorded as 18 or over, those may be shared alongside the fields above while the programme is switched on. They are held separately from what you told us yourself, every row records which partner it came from, and you can ask what is attached to your account, or have it deleted, at privacy@startfromnowhere.com.

That is the whole of it. We have never sold or shared anything else, and the following are excluded at every version of the policy, whatever you switch on: your practice answers and ratings, anything you type into an answer box or an essay, your email address, your name, your payment details, your IP address, your error reports, and your forum posts.

We do not sell or share the personal information of anyone under 18 under any circumstances, and that is enforced in the database rather than by a setting. An account whose age is under 18 cannot appear in the only view an export is permitted to read. The full description is section 9 of the privacy policy.

Checking Your Current Setting

One moment.

This is the switch, not a form: if you are signed in, the button above writes your choice straight to your account and records what you chose and when, and it takes effect on the next export rather than after a review. If you are not signed in, it sets a refusal in this browser that any account you later create on this device starts out honouring, and nothing of yours is in the programme in the meantime, because the programme only ever covers account holders who have switched it on.

Global Privacy Control

We treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as this request, without you having to make it. If your browser or extension sends one, there is nothing further for you to do here.

Making the Request Another Way

You can email privacy@startfromnowhere.com from your account address and we will action it within 15 business days, which is sooner than the law requires. You do not have to give a reason. An authorised agent may make the request on your behalf; we will ask for written permission signed by you and we will verify you directly.

We will not discriminate against you for asking. Price, features, question bank, and the free tier are identical whichever way you choose, and switching this off does not downgrade anything.

One Thing This Cannot Do

Opting out stops future sharing. It cannot recall a copy already delivered to a recipient, because a file that has left our servers is out of our hands. Every recipient is under contract to use the data only for the disclosed purpose and not to re-identify you, and we will pass a deletion request on, but we will not promise you an outcome we do not control. That is why the switch is off by default and why we ask before rather than after.
