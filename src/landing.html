Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers, no fine print games.

What Is Start From Nowhere?

An adaptive exam trainer for the GMAT Focus Edition and the digital SAT. It keeps a live rating for each skill on that exam's official score report, the 12 GMAT skills or the 8 SAT content domains, picks every question at the edge of what you can do, and brings your misses back on a spaced schedule. Practice rounds, mock sections, flashcards, and study games all feed the same engine.

Is It Really Free?

Yes. Everything is free while we build. Paid Plus and Pro plans arrive later for heavy features like unlimited mock sections, and early users keep generous grandfathering. You can practice your first round without even creating an account.

Are These Real Exam Questions?

No, and that is deliberate. Official questions are copyrighted by their test makers, so every one of our {{TOTAL_BANK_ROUND}} items is original, written for Start From Nowhere and mapped to the official skill labels each test maker publishes: the GMAT Focus score report skills, College Board's eight SAT content domains, the ETS GRE content areas, the ACT reporting categories, and the LSAT question types. Pair our adaptive practice with an official practice exam, at mba.com for the GMAT or a Bluebook practice test for the SAT, to calibrate before test day.

How Does the Adaptive Engine Actually Work?

Every question carries a difficulty rating and every skill you train carries a skill rating, chess-style. Answer correctly and your rating rises; miss and it falls. The engine serves questions you should clear about 70% of the time, which research on deliberate practice suggests is the productive zone, and re-serves misses on a spaced schedule.

What Do the Games Do That Practice Rounds Do Not?

Each game trains one specific muscle: Match and Memory build concept recall, Blitz builds speed, Number crunch builds the no-calculator estimation both math sections demand, and the Boss round pressure-tests you on real bank questions at the section pace. Scores come only from accuracy and speed. Nothing is luck-based, and you compete against your own bests, not a leaderboard.

Do I Need an Account?

Not to practice. Your progress saves to your device automatically. A free account adds sync, so your ratings and history follow you across devices. Sign-in is a magic link to your email, no password to manage.

Which Exams Does It Cover?

GMAT Focus Edition, the digital SAT, the GRE General Test, the LSAT and the ACT are live today, each with its own bank, engine settings and analytics.

What Happens to My Data?

Your answers and ratings live on your device first, and in your account only if you create one. From the Account tab you can download everything as a file or delete it entirely, anytime. We do not sell data.
