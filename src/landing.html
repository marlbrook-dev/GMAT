How it works

Every answer updates a rating for the exact skill GMAC reports on your score report, so the trainer and the real test speak the same language.

1

Calibrate

Your first 30 to 40 questions sample every skill at medium difficulty. No guessing about where you stand.

2

Target

Rounds weight your three weakest skills at a level you should clear about 70% of the time. Hard enough to move, not hard enough to stall.

3

Reinforce

Every miss asks why (concept, setup, misread, timing, trap) and comes back on a spaced schedule until it stops being a miss.

Study modes

Ten to twenty-five minutes at a time. Each mode does one job.

Quick 10

Ten adaptive questions across your weakest skills, timed against section pace.

Timed sprint

Fifteen questions with a countdown per question and the bail rule enforced.

Flashcards

Formulas and methods, sorted by what you still miss, back in a day or in sixteen.

Custom drill

Pick sections, skills, question types, difficulty and length. Untimed when you are learning, timed when you are not.

Every Focus format

Problem Solving, Critical Reasoning, Reading Comprehension, Data Sufficiency, Graphics Interpretation, Table Analysis, Two-Part Analysis, Multi-Source Reasoning.

Explanations that start with Why

Each item ships a worked solution and a note on the trap the wrong answers were built around.

Playbook

The methods behind every tracked skill, from the assumption negation test to the DS AD/BCE grid, one page each.

SFN Assist

A plan that studies you back.

Every round states its evidence ("from your last 20 answers"). Every explanation cites the rule it applied. Score bands show the trend and the basis, never a number on its own.
