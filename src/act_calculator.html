How the Composite Is Calculated

Each section is scored from 1 to 36. ACT adds your English, math and reading scores, divides by three and rounds to the nearest whole number, with fractions below one-half rounded down and fractions of one-half or more rounded up.

Three whole numbers always average to a whole number or to one-third or two-thirds past one, so in practice the rule is simpler than it reads: an average ending in .33 rounds down and one ending in .67 rounds up. English 24, math 25 and reading 25 add to 74, and 74 divided by 3 is 24.67, a Composite of 25. English 24, math 24 and reading 25 add to 73, which is 24.33, a Composite of 24.
