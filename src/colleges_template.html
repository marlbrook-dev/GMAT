Two things to know before you read the table. Admission rate and test scores are shown on every row and are deliberately not scored: rewarding a school for rejecting people measures demand, not teaching. And net price for a public university is the in-state figure, because that is the only one the Scorecard publishes. Public institutions therefore score better on cost than an out-of-state student would actually experience; if you would pay out-of-state, read the tuition columns instead of the cost component.
