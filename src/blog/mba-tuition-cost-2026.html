How much does an MBA cost? At twelve two-year programs in our school library, including Harvard, Stanford and Wharton, tuition alone for 2026-27 runs from $83,700 a year at Duke Fuqua to $92,658 at NYU Stern (school tuition pages, 2026). Fees, health insurance and living costs come on top. Public programs charge residents of their state less, and some schools publish one price for the whole program instead of a yearly rate.

What Does Tuition Leave Out?

Tuition is one line of the bill. Wharton adds a General Fee of $4,268 and a Clinical Fee of $770, for $93,008 in tuition and fees (Wharton, 2026). Dartmouth Tuck adds a program fee of $6,150 (Tuck, 2026), and Harvard Business School lists a student health fee of $1,944 and a student health insurance plan of $4,954 beside its tuition (HBS, 2026). Chicago Booth adds a student services fee of $1,560 and a first-year administrative service fee of $3,100 (Chicago Booth, 2026).

Living costs are the largest addition. NYU Stern puts the total cost of attendance for a first-year student at $140,668 (NYU Stern, 2026), Yale SOM gives a one-year cost of attendance of $128,976 (Yale SOM, 2026), and Carnegie Mellon estimates $120,798 for a single graduate student at Tepper (CMU, 2026). A school's cost of attendance page is the closer guide to a budget; the tuition line is the part every school prints in the same way.

Why Do Public MBA Programs Have Two Prices?

A state university usually charges residents of its state less than students from elsewhere, so a public program's tuition depends on where you live.

How Should You Compare MBA Costs?

  • Compare like with like: a year of tuition with a year of tuition, and a whole-program figure with another whole-program figure.
  • Read what each figure covers. Tuition only, tuition and fees, and a program fee folded into tuition are three different numbers.
  • Check which residency a public program's figure is for, and whether your state or country qualifies for the lower rate.
  • Budget from the cost of attendance page, which adds fees, insurance and living costs, and remember that each year's tuition is set separately: Emory Goizueta says its rates are updated each spring for the upcoming academic year (Goizueta, 2026).
