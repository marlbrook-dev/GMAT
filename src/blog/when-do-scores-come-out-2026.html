GMAT official scores should be available within five days, and GRE official scores 8 to 10 days after the test (mba.com, 2026; ETS, 2026). The LSAT, the SAT and the ACT release scores on published dates. When Do GMAT Scores Come Out?

Your official score report should be available within five days (mba.com, 2026). GMAC adds that, although not typical, it can take up to 20 days for an exam to be scored, and that scores cannot be expedited, so paying or asking cannot bring a score forward. If you are close to an application deadline, count on the 20 days rather than the five.

The timing also matters for score reports. Up to five score reports are free if you choose the programs within 48 hours of your Official Score becoming available, and each additional report costs $35 in the United States (mba.com, 2026). Our guide to what the GMAT costs covers the rest of the fees.

When Do GRE Scores Come Out?

Your official GRE General Test scores are available in your ETS account 8 to 10 days after your test date, and ETS emails you when they are (ETS, 2026). At the same time, ETS sends an official score report to each score recipient you chose on test day. ETS's scheduling page adds that schools may receive scores faster depending on the delivery method they have chosen, that electronic scores go to schools twice a week, and that you should allow time for delivery and for each school to process your scores when you pick a test date.

The release date holds only if you have an approved writing sample on file and no holds on your account. A first-time test taker must complete LSAT Argumentative Writing and have it approved before a score can be released. It opens in JD Services eight days before each administration, and processing can take up to three weeks from the day you complete it, so LSAC strongly recommends completing it as soon as it opens (LSAC, 2026). A writing sample from the current reportable score period, as early as July 2021, still counts.

When Do ACT Scores Come Out?

ACT says over 97 percent of ACT scores are released within about 2 to 4 weeks after the test date, and that scores are posted as soon as they have been processed and validated (ACT, 2026). Most students can see them in their MyACT account. If you took the optional writing test, complete scores, writing included, may come a bit later, because all the parts are processed together. ACT's page on online testing gives wider ranges: multiple-choice scores in two to eight weeks, and five to eight weeks with the writing test (ACT, 2026).

How to Plan Around an Application Deadline

Work back from the deadline with each test maker's longest stated wait, not its usual one. That is 20 days for the GMAT, 10 days for the GRE, the published release date for the LSAT and the SAT, and for the ACT up to eight weeks, the widest range ACT gives. Then add the time a school needs to receive and process your scores, which ETS and College Board both leave to the school. For the LSAT, complete the writing sample as soon as it opens, since the score waits for it. If a second attempt might follow, our guide to how many times you can take each exam sets out the waiting periods between attempts.

While you wait, the free trainers for the GMAT, GRE, LSAT, SAT and ACT run an adaptive round with no account needed, and report an estimated range from your practice, never a predicted score.
