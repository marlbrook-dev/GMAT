The GMAT costs $275 at a test center in the United States and $300 when it is delivered online (mba.com, 2026). Up to five score reports are free if you choose the programs within 48 hours of your Official Score becoming available, and each report after that costs $35. Rescheduling and cancelling cost more the closer the appointment is, and nothing can be changed in the last 24 hours. Here is the full US fee table, and what each fee means for your plans.

Prices are set by location. The payment page shows fees only once you choose a country, and the price depends on where your test center is, or where you are for the online exam, with local taxes (mba.com, 2026). If you are testing outside the United States, look up your own country's table before you set a budget. Online delivery costs $25 more than a test center in the US.

How Much Does It Cost to Reschedule the GMAT?

When you rescheduleAt a test centerOnline
More than 60 days before the appointment$55$60
15 to 60 days before$110$120
14 days or less before$165$180

The fee triples between the first window and the last. Within 14 days of the appointment, a reschedule costs 60 percent of the exam fee at either kind of appointment (mba.com, 2026). So a move you can see coming is cheapest made early: if your practice results say you need another month, the time to move the date is more than 60 days out, not the week before. A change made by phone adds a $10.00 service fee (GMAC, 2026).

What Refund Do You Get If You Cancel the GMAT?

When you cancelAt a test centerOnline
More than 60 days before the appointment$110 refund$120 refund
15 to 60 days before$80 refund$90 refund
14 days or less before$55 refund$60 refund

A cancellation never returns the whole fee. More than 60 days out, the refund is 40 percent of the fee at either kind of appointment; within 14 days it is 20 percent. From 15 to 60 days it is 30 percent of the online fee and about 29 percent of the test center fee (mba.com, 2026).

Compare the two before you decide. Within 14 days of a test center appointment, rescheduling costs $165 and keeps you registered, while cancelling returns $55 of your $275. If you still mean to take the exam, rescheduling is usually the better use of the money.

What Happens in the Last 24 Hours?

An appointment cannot be rescheduled or cancelled within 24 hours of its start, and an exam that was not rescheduled or cancelled more than 24 hours ahead forfeits the entire fee (mba.com, 2026). If something might keep you from testing, make the call before that window closes.

How Much Do GMAT Score Reports Cost?

Up to five score reports are free if you choose the programs within 48 hours of your Official Score becoming available (mba.com, 2026). After that, or for more programs, each additional score report costs $35, whether you tested at a test center or online. Score reporting fees are non-refundable, and a report cannot be cancelled once it has been sent, so settle your list before the 48 hours start. Additional reports can go to as many programs as you like while your score is no more than five years old (mba.com, 2026).

Can You Get a GMAT Fee Waiver?

A fee waiver lets an eligible candidate register at a reduced cost or no cost. GMAT fee waivers are typically distributed through participating business schools and approved programs, and the organization that issues one sets who qualifies (mba.com, 2026). Vouchers work the same way and can come from business schools, scholarship organizations, test prep organizations and GMAC Customer Care. Either one applies to the exam fee only: it cannot pay a rescheduling fee, and it is not refunded, except as required by law (GMAC, 2026). If a program on your list offers waivers, ask its admissions office before you pay.

Someone else can also pay. GMAC accepts another person's credit card, such as a family member's, employer's or sponsor's, with the cardholder's permission (mba.com, 2026).

How Can You Keep the Cost of the GMAT Down?

  • Leave room in your schedule. A move more than 60 days out costs $55 or $60; one within 14 days costs $165 or $180.
  • Choose your programs within 48 hours of your score, while five reports are free.
  • Ask the programs you are applying to about fee waivers before you register.
  • Practice before you pay. The GMAT trainer gives you a first round free, with no account needed, and reports an estimated score range from your practice, never a predicted score.
