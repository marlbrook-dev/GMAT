The LSAT costs $253 for the August 2026 to June 2027 testing year, and that fee includes LSAT Argumentative Writing (LSAC, 2026). The test is only part of what applicants pay LSAC: the Credential Assembly Service (CAS) costs $219, and each law school application needs its own law school report at $45. So the LSAT, CAS and reports for five schools come to $697 in LSAC fees. Here is every fee, the deadlines that decide what a change costs, and how fee waivers work.

A law school report is either a CAS Law School Report or an LSAT-Only Law School Report. Some schools require the first; others accept the second (LSAC, 2026). Check each school's requirement before you order.

The last step costs $253, as much as the test itself (LSAC, 2026). If your preparation is behind, the registration deadline is the date to decide by.

Can You Get a Refund for the LSAT?

Yes, a full one, if you ask in time. You can request a full refund of the registration fee through the test's full refund deadline, which is generally the same as its registration deadline. After that, you can still withdraw by 11:59 p.m. ET the day before you test, which keeps an absentee notation off your LSAC file, but you get no refund. A registration with no testing time scheduled through Prometric by the scheduling deadline is withdrawn automatically, also without a refund (LSAC, 2026).

Some fees are never refunded: the test date change, LSAT Score Preview and Score Audit fees, and, since May 20, 2024, the CAS fee. If you registered with a fee waiver or coupon and are refunded in an eligible period, the waiver or coupon goes back into your account for a future test (LSAC, 2026).

Can You Get an LSAT Fee Waiver?

LSAC's fee waiver is for US and Canadian applicants, in two tiers set by income against the federal poverty guidelines (LSAC, 2026):

  • Tier 1: two LSATs, including LSAT Argumentative Writing and LSAT Score Preview, 6 law school reports, a five-year CAS subscription and a one-year LawHub Advantage subscription.
  • Tier 2: one LSAT with the same inclusions, 3 law school reports, the five-year CAS subscription and LawHub Advantage.

The benefits arrive in steps: the LSAT once you complete 2 LSAT PrepTests in LawHub in Exam Mode, and CAS once you complete LawHub's "How Do I Apply to Law School?" course. LSAC recommends applying at least six months before the registration deadline you are aiming for and typically decides within two to five business days (LSAC, 2026).

How Can You Keep the Cost of the LSAT Down?

  • Decide on your test date by the registration deadline, when a change is free.
  • Check the fee waiver first if your income may qualify, since Tier 1 covers two LSATs, CAS and 6 law school reports.
  • Confirm which report each school needs before you order.
  • Practice before you pay for a second sitting. The LSAT trainer gives you a first round free, with no account needed, and reports an estimated range from your practice, never a predicted score.
