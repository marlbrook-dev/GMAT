A full-time MBA class can hold fewer than 30 students or close to 1,000. Across 41 programs in our school library with a published size for a class that entered in 2025 or 2026, the class runs from 29 students at UC Riverside to 982 at Columbia Business School, which counts its August and January intakes together, and the middle program, Vanderbilt Owen, has 160 (school class profiles and Poets&Quants, 2025 and 2026). 13 of the 41 have 300 or more students, and 15 have fewer than 100.

Why Columbia's Count Is the Largest

Columbia Business School admits students in two intakes, and its figure counts both. Poets&Quants' report on the class gives 982 students across the August 2025 and January 2026 cohorts, of whom 758 entered in August (Poets&Quants, 2025). The August intake alone would sit between Wharton's 888 and Chicago Booth's 635 in the table. Other figures count only part of a school's full-time students: Northwestern Kellogg's 534 is its incoming Two-Year MBA class and Emory Goizueta's 128 its Two-Year MBA, while MIT Sloan's 450 includes students in its LGO program (school class profiles, 2025 and 2026).

Class Size Is Not an Acceptance Rate

A class size counts the students who enrolled, not the applicants a school admitted, so it cannot be turned into an acceptance rate. Columbia admitted an estimated 1,450 of 7,477 applicants, and 982 enrolled (Poets&Quants estimate, 2025). Where a school prints its applicants or applications beside its class size, the two show how many there were for each place in the class: Stanford GSB's class profile gives 434 new students and 7,259 total applicants, about 17 applicants for each new student; UCLA Anderson's gives 287 students and 2,422 applications, about 8 for each; and Georgetown McDonough's gives 197 enrolled and 1,269 applications, about 6 for each (school class profiles, 2025 and 2026). Each of those divides the applicants or applications the school reports by the students it enrolled, rounded to a whole number.

How Should You Use These Numbers?

  • Check what a school counts: one intake or two, one full-time format or all of them.
  • Check which class a figure describes, since a class that entered in 2025 and one that entered in 2026 are different classes.
  • Read the size beside the rest of the profile: average GPA for MBA programs, MBA work experience by school and how to read an MBA class profile cover the numbers that sit next to it.

The Bottom Line

An MBA class in our library can be 29 students or 982, and the middle program, Vanderbilt Owen, enrolled 160.
