The SAT costs $68 to register in the United States (College Board, 2026). Outside the United States it costs $68 plus a $43 international fee, $111 in all, and some test centers add a $24 fee. The first 4 score reports are free if you order them within 9 days of the test, and a fee waiver makes two SATs free. Here is every other fee College Board charges, and what a change of plans costs.

The 9-day window is the one to plan around. Reports ordered by 9 days after the test date are free for up to 4 colleges; the same reports ordered later cost $15 each (College Board, 2026). If you know where you are applying, choose those colleges before test day.

What Does It Cost to Change or Cancel the SAT?

You can change your test center for $34. To change the test date, College Board has you cancel and register for a new test (College Board, 2026).

Cancelling gives back more than you might expect. If you cancel by Thursday 11:59 p.m. ET before test day, you get the full registration fee back and are charged a cancellation fee: $34 by the change deadline, or $44 after it. So cancelling in time returns $34 of the $68, and a late cancellation $24. Refunds go to the original form of payment and are processed beginning 3 to 5 business days after you cancel. Score reports you ordered with your registration are refundable only if you are absent on test day, and rush score reports cannot be refunded (College Board, 2026).

What Does an SAT Fee Waiver Cover?

If you qualify for a fee waiver, College Board gives you (College Board, 2026):

  • Two free SAT tests, with no late registration fees for them.
  • Unlimited score reports to send to colleges.
  • Waived application fees at participating colleges, if you are a senior in high school and have taken the SAT.
  • Free CSS Profile applications for financial aid from participating schools.
  • No cancellation fees, with your unused fee waiver benefits returned when you cancel.
  • No international fees for free tests if you are a US student testing abroad, and reduced fees for score verification reports.

Check College Board's fee waiver eligibility rules before you register, since a waiver changes almost every number above.

How Does the SAT's Cost Compare?

Of the five tests this site covers, the SAT costs the least to register for: the ACT is $70, the GRE $249, the LSAT $253 and the GMAT $275 at a US test center.
