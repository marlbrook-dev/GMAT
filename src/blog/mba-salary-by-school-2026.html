The median salary for MBA graduates of 2025 runs from $105,000 to $185,000 across the 26 programs in our school library that publish one: from Rutgers at the low end to Stanford GSB and Wharton at the top, with half of the 26 at $175,000 or more (school employment reports and Poets&Quants, 2025 and 2026). A median is the pay of the graduate in the middle of those who reported one: half earned more, half less.

What Does a Median Salary Tell You?

It tells you about graduates who accepted a job and reported their pay, which is not the whole class. Yale SOM's figure comes from the 91.8 percent of students reporting accepted offers who included salary information, Kellogg's from the 83 percent of job-accepting graduates who reported usable salary information, and Maryland Smith's from 16 graduates, 47.1 percent of those who accepted a job (school employment reports, 2025). The fewer graduates behind a median, the more one or two salaries can move it. Where a school prints a spread, it shows more than the middle: at Yale SOM, a quarter of those reporting earned $140,000 or less and a quarter $190,000 or more (Yale SOM, 2025).

Which Industries Pay MBA Graduates the Most?

Finance and consulting, at the schools that break pay down by industry. At Stanford GSB, graduates who went into finance had a median base salary of $200,000; at MIT Sloan, the highest medians were in consulting ($190,000), manufacturing ($180,000) and finance ($175,000); and at Kellogg, where consulting took 38 percent of accepted offers, its median base salary was $190,000 (school employment reports, 2025). Kellogg's career outcomes page shows the range behind each industry's median as well.

Base Salary, Signing Bonus and Total Compensation

A base salary leaves out the signing bonus, and most schools report the two apart. For the Class of 2025, all seven M7 schools, the group Poets&Quants tracks as Chicago Booth, Columbia, Harvard, Kellogg, MIT Sloan, Stanford and Wharton, reported a $30,000 median signing bonus, and Poets&Quants' M7 report finds just $10,000 between their highest and lowest median base salaries (Poets&Quants, 2026). Dartmouth Tuck and Duke Fuqua report the same $30,000 median bonus. Kellogg's and Emory Goizueta's median total compensation is $205,000, $30,000 above their median base salaries (school employment reports, 2025). How Should You Use These Numbers?

  • Compare a median with a median, and check which graduates it describes and how many reported.
  • Look past the headline: industry, function and location medians can sit well above or below it.
  • Set pay beside cost and hiring: how much an MBA costs and how to read MBA employment rates cover the other two numbers a return depends on.
