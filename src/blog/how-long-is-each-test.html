The GRE is the shortest of the five at about 1 hour 58 minutes (ETS, 2026). The SAT takes 2 hours 14 minutes and the GMAT 2 hours 15, and each allows a 10-minute break (College Board, 2026; mba.com, 2025). The ACT runs 2 hours 45 minutes with its optional science section, and the LSAT about 3 hours (ACT, 2026; LSAC, 2026). Here is how each exam divides its time, and how long you get per question.

How Long Is the GMAT?

2 hours 15 minutes, plus one optional 10-minute break (mba.com, 2025). The GMAT Focus Edition has three sections of 45 minutes each: Quantitative Reasoning with 21 questions, Verbal Reasoning with 23 and Data Insights with 20, 64 questions in all, with no writing assessment (GMAC, 2026). You choose the order in which you take the three sections, and within a section you can bookmark questions and change up to 3 answers (mba.com, 2025). Our guide to what the GMAT costs covers booking it.

How Long Is the GRE?

About 1 hour 58 minutes, in five sections (ETS, 2026). Analytical Writing always comes first: one Analyze an Issue task in 30 minutes. The Verbal Reasoning and Quantitative Reasoning sections follow in any order, each measure in two sections:

How Long Is the LSAT?

LSAC says about 3 hours for standard test takers (LSAC, 2026). The test is four 35-minute multiple-choice sections, 140 minutes in all, with a 10-minute intermission between the second and third. Two sections are scored Logical Reasoning and one is scored Reading Comprehension; the fourth is an unscored section of either type, and it can come at any point. LSAC does not publish how many questions each section has, so this guide does not give one. LSAT Argumentative Writing is an unscored writing sample, administered separately from the multiple-choice sections, and the law school report LSAC sends includes your LSAT scores and writing sample (LSAC, 2026). Our LSAT format and scoring guide covers each section.

How Long Is the SAT?

2 hours 14 minutes of testing for 98 questions, with a 10-minute break between the two sections (College Board, 2026). Reading and Writing takes 64 minutes in two 32-minute modules, 54 questions in all, and Math takes 70 minutes in two 35-minute modules, 44 questions in all. The test is adaptive by section: how you do in the first module decides whether the second is harder or easier. Our digital SAT format guide covers the modules in detail.

How Long Is the ACT?

With the optional science section, 2 hours 45 minutes of timed testing for 171 multiple-choice questions (ACT, 2026). Without it, English, math and reading add up to 125 minutes, 2 hours 5 minutes, for 131 questions. The optional writing essay adds 40 minutes after a 5-minute break.
