The GRE General Test costs $249 in all areas of the world except China, where it is $231.30, and India, where the ETS India website sets the fee (ETS, 2026). In the United States two more items shape what you pay: ETS's final charge includes a non-refundable 4% online service fee, and sales or similar taxes are added to the order (ETS, 2026). The fee covers up to four score recipients. Here is every other GRE fee, and the deadline that decides what a change costs.

What Is Added to the GRE Fee?

Two things change what you pay. First, the final charge for every transaction includes a non-refundable 4% online service fee. Second, in the United States, and in Australia, Canada, Chile, Colombia, the European Union countries, Nigeria, Norway, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Vietnam and Singapore, GRE fees exclude sales, use, value-added or similar taxes, which are added to the order when you place it (GRE Information Bulletin 2026-27, ETS). Check the total at checkout rather than budgeting on the listed fee alone.

How Much Does It Cost to Reschedule or Cancel the GRE?

The GRE has one deadline for both. You must reschedule or cancel no later than four days before your test date, or 10 days in Mainland China. ETS's own example: the deadline for a Saturday appointment at a US testing location is Tuesday (ETS, 2026).

  • Rescheduling before the deadline costs $55, or $53.90 in China, about 22 percent of the $249 fee. A test not rescheduled by the deadline forfeits its fee.
  • Cancelling before the deadline refunds half the original test fee, $124.50 of $249, automatically. The refund applies only to a cancellation made by the deadline, and service fees are not refunded.
  • Changing your test center costs $55.

The GMAT works differently: its fees rise in three steps as the appointment nears, and our guide to what the GMAT costs lays them out. If you are still choosing between the two tests, see GMAT vs GRE for MBA admissions.

How Much Do GRE Score Reports Cost?

Your test fee covers up to four score recipients. You choose them at the end of the test, after you see your unofficial scores, along with the ScoreSelect option of sending your most recent scores or all of them (ETS, 2026). If you choose no recipients then, send scores later, or want more than four, each additional score report costs $40 per recipient. So the cheapest plan is to know your list before test day.

What Do GRE Score Reviews and Reinstatements Cost?

A score review of the Analytical Writing measure costs $60 and must be requested within 90 days of your test; allow about four weeks for the result, and note that your scores cannot go to recipients until it is done. If you cancel your scores and change your mind, you can reinstate them within 60 days of the test date for $50 (ETS, 2026). New York State residents can also buy a Question-and-Answer review of the Verbal and Quantitative sections for $50.

Can You Get a GRE Fee Reduction?

Yes, if you qualify. ETS's Fee Reduction Program lets a voucher holder register for the General Test for $100, with free access to POWERPREP PLUS Online Practice Tests 1 and 2 (valued at $45 each) and ScoreItNow! Online Writing Practice (valued at $20) (ETS, 2026). Eligibility and how to apply are on ETS's fee reduction page.

What Does a GRE Retake Cost?

ETS allows the General Test once every 21 days, up to five times in any continuous rolling 12-month period (ETS, 2026). Before you pay for a second attempt, check where your scores stand: our guide to what a good GRE score is puts them against ETS's percentiles. The GRE trainer gives you a first round free, with no account needed, and reports an estimated range from your practice, never a predicted score.
