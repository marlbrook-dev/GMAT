Two of the five tests have no registration deadline in the usual sense. GMAC offers the GMAT at test centers throughout the year, with online appointments available 24/7 in many regions (GMAC, 2026), and ETS asks only that you register for the GRE at least two calendar days ahead online (ETS, 2026). The LSAT, the SAT and the ACT are given on fixed dates, and registration closes weeks before each one: for the LSAT at a single deadline per administration, and for the SAT and the ACT with a late period that costs extra (LSAC, 2026; College Board, 2026; ACT, 2026). GMAT: No Deadline, But Seats Go

You register for the GMAT by booking an appointment, not by meeting a deadline. GMAC offers the exam at test centers around the world throughout the year, and test center availability depends on your location, seat availability and local holidays and operating schedules. Online appointments are available 24/7 in many regions, which may give you more flexibility (GMAC, 2026).

The catch is supply. GMAC warns that availability can change quickly, especially during peak admissions periods, and suggests widening your search radius or trying other dates and nearby cities if nothing near you is open (GMAC, 2026). Booking late also leaves less room to change course: an appointment cannot be switched between test center and online, so a change of mind means canceling and booking again, and cancellation fees may apply (GMAC, 2026).

GRE: Two Days' Notice

ETS asks you to register online at least two calendar days before your preferred test date, or, if you register by phone, at least two business days before it (ETS, 2026). Changing your mind has its own deadline: you must reschedule no later than four days before your test date, or 10 days in Mainland China, or the test fee is forfeited, and there is a fee to reschedule (ETS, 2026).

The real limit is usually your application deadline. ETS says your score report arrives 8 to 10 days after your test date, that electronic scores go to schools twice a week, and that a test date should leave time for delivery and for the school's own processing (ETS, 2026). LSAT: One Deadline Per Administration

Each LSAT administration has a single registration deadline, weeks before its first testing day, and LSAC lists every date in Eastern Time, with all receipt deadlines by 11:59 p.m. ET (LSAC, 2026). For the administrations after this post:

Those are the United States and Canada dates; the international April and June 2027 administrations share the same registration deadlines (LSAC, 2026). Registration and scheduling are separate steps: registered test takers receive an email when scheduling becomes available (LSAC, 2026). The deadline also closes two other things. The accommodation request deadline is always the same as the registration deadline, with no exceptions, and changing to another test date is free only through the registration deadline, then $153 for up to 7 days after it and $253 from 8 days after it through the day before testing (LSAC, 2026).

If You Need Accommodations, the Deadline Comes Sooner

For two of these tests the registration deadline is also the last day to ask for accommodations: LSAC's accommodation request deadline is always the registration deadline, and from the June 2026 test event ACT's is the regular registration deadline, appeals included (LSAC, 2026; ACT, 2026). The GMAT, GRE and SAT all ask for approval before you book or test, and their reviews take weeks, as our guide to testing accommodations explains.

Whichever test you are preparing for, the free trainers for the GMAT, GRE, LSAT, SAT and ACT run an adaptive round with no account needed, and report an estimated range from your practice, never a predicted score.
