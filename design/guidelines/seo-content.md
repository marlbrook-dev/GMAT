# SEO content strategy — The Study Room (blog)

Purpose: organic traffic via high-intent exam-prep queries. Written for whoever (human or Claude) generates posts — follow this and every post lands on-brand and on-keyword.

## Keyword pillars (2026 research)

1. **Format & changes** — "GMAT Focus Edition", "GMAT format 2026", "new GMAT sections", "Data Insights section". The 2024 format change (IR + essay removed; three equally-weighted sections, 805-point scale) still drives heavy search volume because older materials are outdated.
2. **Timelines & plans** — "how long to study for the GMAT", "GMAT study plan", "8 week GMAT study schedule", "GMAT study plan for working professionals". Structured 6–12 week plans with 50–100 point gains are the credible claim range.
3. **Comparisons & decisions** — "GMAT vs GRE", "best GMAT prep course", "is the GMAT hard", "GMAT waiver". Comparison pages earn links; be even-handed, never trash competitors.
4. **Scores** — "good GMAT score", "GMAT score chart", "average GMAT score by school", "GMAT percentiles 2026". Table-heavy pages; update yearly, date in title.
5. **Tactics** — "data sufficiency tips", "GMAT time management", "critical reasoning strategies", "GMAT mock test free". One tactic per post, demonstrated with a worked example.
6. **Study science** — "spaced repetition", "dual coding", "active recall" — differentiator content tying methods to product mechanics.
7. Repeat pillars 1–5 per exam as we launch them (GRE, LSAT, MCAT, EA).

## Post template (SEO mechanics)

- Title ≤ 60 chars, keyword first half, year when relevant. Meta description ≤ 155 chars with the keyword.
- One H1; question-phrased H2s (they win featured snippets); answer the title's question in the first 80 words.
- Include one stat or table per post; internal-link to the relevant exam page + 2 sibling posts; end with one CTA (free first round).
- FAQ block (3–5 Q&As) at the bottom with FAQPage structured data.
- Author + date + updated date visible. Categories: Exam Guides, Study Science, Success Stories, Company News.
- Claims discipline: cite ranges we can defend; never invent stats about our own results.

## Reader submissions

Blog page has a "Share your story" form → editors@startfromnowhere.com. Review flow: editor reads within a week, replies either way; published pieces get author credit and pre-publication approval of edits. Success stories are the trust engine — prioritize them.
